# Draft: Youghall Beach

Category: outdoor
Area: Acadie-Bathurst
Tags: beach | park | boardwalk | swimming | waterfront

## Notes
Youghall Beach park north of Acadie-Bathurst near Gowen Brae and the CCNB Youghall Campus.
City of Bathurst describes it as a beach park with free access to warm waters, groomed sandy beaches, a scenic boardwalk, and an easy boat slip.
Summer operations on the city page: 8:00am to 8:00pm daily, free parking, lifeguards 10:00am to 5:30pm, designated swimming zone, beach sand cleaned daily 7:00am to 10:00am, floating wheelchairs available through East Coast Adventures, no overnight camping, and pets permitted on leash.
The city page also says no swimming on the part of the beach nearest the channel.
