# Bootlegger

Bar and patio on Main St near La Louga Night Club and La Promenade Waterfront.

## Public notes
- Services include local draft and brew, food, a well-received popcorn machine, and a beautiful outdoor terrace for nice summer days.
- It is part of the summer event circuit for Block Party and Hospitality Days.
- Also close to La Promenade Waterfront.

## Contact
- Address: 100 Main St, Bathurst, NB
