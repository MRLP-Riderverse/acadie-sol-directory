# Draft: Youghall Beach

## Notes
Youghall Beach, north Acadie-Bathurst by Gowen Brae and the CCNB Youghall Campus. City of Bathurst describes it as Youghall Beach Park with free access to warm waters, groomed sandy beaches, a scenic boardwalk, and an easy boat slip. Summer operations listed on the city page: 8:00am to 8:00pm daily, free parking, lifeguards 10:00am to 5:30pm, designated swimming zone (no fishing), beach sand cleaned daily 7:00am to 10:00am, floating wheelchairs available through East Coast Adventures, no overnight camping, and pets permitted on leash. The city page also says no swimming on part of the beach nearest the channel.
