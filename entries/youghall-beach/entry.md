# Youghall Beach

Youghall Beach park north of Acadie-Bathurst near Gowen Brae and the CCNB Youghall Campus.

## Résumé français
Parc de plage Youghall au nord d’Acadie-Bathurst, avec promenade et zone de baignade désignée.

## Public notes
City of Bathurst describes it as a beach park with free access to warm waters, groomed sandy beaches, a scenic boardwalk, and an easy boat slip.
The city page also says no swimming on the part of the beach nearest the channel.

## Contact
- Hours: Summer operations on the city page: 8:00am to 8:00pm daily, free parking, lifeguards 10:00am to 5:30pm, designated swimming zone, beach sand cleaned daily 7:00am to 10:00am, floating wheelchairs available through East Coast Adventures, no overnight camping, and pets permitted on leash.
