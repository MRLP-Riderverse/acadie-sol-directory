# Acadie.sol

Acadie.sol is a digitally based Acadian community project, social club, and cultural media space stewarded by MidnightRider.sol. Projet Pilote Acadien - 2026 is inspired by web3 and rooted in community-shaped cultural work from Acadie-Bathurst.

## Public notes

Acadie.sol is based digitally in Bathurst and has no civic address. It is a local-first project for Acadian arts, media, community connection, and experiments in web3-inspired public infrastructure.
