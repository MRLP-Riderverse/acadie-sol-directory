# Draft: Bootlegger

Category: bar
Area: Acadie-Bathurst
Tags: bar | patio | nightlife | restaurant

## Notes
Bootlegger on Main St in Acadie-Bathurst.
It sits in front of the TD Bank and La Louga Night Club, and is also next door to other local shops that can be added later.
Services include local draft and brew, food, a well-received popcorn machine, and a beautiful outdoor terrace for nice summer days.
It is part of the summer event circuit for Block Party and Hospitality Days.
Also close to La Promenade Waterfront.
